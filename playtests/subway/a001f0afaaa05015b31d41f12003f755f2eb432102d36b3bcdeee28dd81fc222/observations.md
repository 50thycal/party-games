# Owner observations

Calvin–Zoe human multiplayer playtest, room DGLE, rules "v.6-ish" (state version 25). Owner
comments, consolidated from the shared notes; interpretation and rules live in analysis.md.

- A device that closes out for about twenty minutes should not reopen straight into the game
  weeks later.
- Player pads on the iPad should show small symbols for each company's Engineering cards by
  category (line, station, neighborhood), with a unique tiny icon per card sharing a family
  theme, and abbreviated chips for Destination cards (S for Stadium, A for Airport).
- After "I am <company>" on the iPad, flash a quick summary of what happened on the opponents'
  turns (cash received and anything relevant), dismissable.
- Consider paying cash for completed Destination cards to help cash flow; if so, debt needs
  limiting or there is too much cash.
- Debt lever: the owner went aggressively into debt on a turn where two lines were about to
  finish, paid for three crews, and was out of debt by the end of that turn from completion cash.
  Possibly overpowered. Proposed: debt only on a later turn, not the turn you build.
- Debt and Destination cash are the biggest comments of this series; review the gameplay data.
- A single button on the iPad to buy an extra Engineering or Destination card, choosing which
  in a pop-up, drawn at random.
- Phone Destination page: the met indicator appeared only on the bottom card, not the top; the
  cards are too busy and should be slimmer: label, pictures, station names, nothing else.
- Add a small "R" round badge to the phone header.
